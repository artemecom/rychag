 Sweden: streaming bundles cut churn by nearly half despite lower satisfaction
 Just 14% of bundled streaming subscriptions were cancelled over the past year, compared with 25% of standalone services, as Sweden’s paid streaming market reached 11.2 million subscriptions in Q3 2026.
 by Señal News

 @senalnews
 23 OCT 2026
 Share
 Mediavision’s latest analysis of the Swedish TV and streaming market highlights the retention advantage of bundled subscriptions, despite lower customer satisfaction. Over the past year, 14% of bundled subscriptions were cancelled, compared with 25% of standalone subscriptions.
However, standalone services scored higher on satisfaction. On a seven-point scale, 63% of standalone subscriptions received ratings of six or seven, against 50% for bundled services. “One possible explanation is that households assess the bundle as a whole rather than each streaming service individually,” said Adrian Grande, Principal Analyst at Mediavision.
Cancellations do not necessarily mean permanent customer losses. A total of 3.4 million streaming subscriptions were cancelled over the past year, with one in four subsequently reactivated. For just over half of those returning subscriptions, content was cited as one reason for rejoining. Specific films and series were mentioned about as often as promotional offers or discounts.
“A particular film or series, or access to specific sports rights, can prompt households to take out a streaming subscription,” Grande said. “The challenge is retaining customers once the series ends or the sports season is over.” He added that bundling may then play a greater role than satisfaction with an individual service.
The findings come as Sweden’s paid streaming market continues to expand. The number of paid subscriptions reached 11.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 18% year-on-year, underlining the importance of retention strategies as competition for subscribers intensifies.
