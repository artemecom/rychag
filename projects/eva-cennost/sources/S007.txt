What is Call Filter?

Review full answer

Call Filter screens incoming callers to alert you if a number may be spam, a robocall or fraud. Call Filter detects spam and blocks high-risk spam calls by forwarding to voicemail.

If you're on a standard monthly plan or prepaid plan and have a compatible device, Call Filter (free) is included on your account.

Call Filter (free) gives:

Spam Detection

Spam Filter

Spam and blocked call log

Allow numbers

Report spam

Upgrade to Call Filter Plus for even more features:

Caller ID

Spam lookup

Personal block list

Block by category

Manage features with the Verizon Protect app or Call Filter in My Verizon.

What does Call Filter cost?

Review full answer

Call Filter is free* for Verizon customers with a compatible device. You can also get:

Call Filter Plus for $3.99/month.

Call Filter Plus (multi-line) for $10.99/month (for 3 or more lines).

Call Filter Plus tools are bundled directly into Verizon's device protection packages, such as Verizon Mobile Protect.

Keep in mind: Devices that aren't compatible with the Verizon Protect app may not be able to upgrade to Call Filter Plus.

*Data usage applies.

How do I get Call Filter?

Get Call Filter

Standard monthly or prepaid plans with compatible devices are eligible to get Call Filter for free.

Preloaded on most Android phones.

Download the Verizon Protect app from Apple App Store or Google Play, if not preloaded.

Minimum requirements - Android 10.0 or iOS 16.6.

Review full answer

If you're on a standard monthly plan or prepaid plan and have a compatible device, Call Filter (free) is included on your account:

The Verizon Protect app is pre-loaded on most Android™* devices and may already be on your phone.

If it's not on your phone, you can download the Verizon Protect app from the Apple® App Store® or Google Play™.

Good to know:

Android phones require Android 10.0 or higher and iPhones require iOS 16.6 or later.

Some devices aren't compatible with the Verizon Protect app. Check device compatiblibility. If your device isn't compatible with the app, you can manage Call Filter in My Verizon instead. Visit the Products & plan perks page in the My Verizon app or website to turn on Call Filter.

Is Call Filter supported on Dual SIM capable iPhones?

Review full answer

Call Filter is available on lines with Dual SIM-capable devices when the eSIM and physical Verizon SIM are active on the device at the same time.

If your line has a Dual SIM-capable phone with:

Only one SIM activated (i.e., eSIM or physical SIM) — You can use the Verizon Protect app and manage Call Filter through My Verizon (app and website).

Both SIMs active on Verizon — You can add Call Filter on both lines through My Verizon. However, the Verizon Protect app is eligible with only one SIM at a time. During setup or at any time through the Account tab, you can choose which SIM you want to use with the Verizon Protect app.

How does Call Filter detect spam?

Review full answer

Call Filter uses analytics and databases that are constantly evolving to detect spam calls, including those that may be spoofed.

You can help us improve the accuracy of the service by reporting spam numbers we may have missed.

Verizon doesn't guarantee that all spam calls will be detected. If you disagree with how a number was categorized by the system, you can unmark the number as spam, add to your contacts, or allow the number.

What if a legitimate number is identified as spam?

Review full answer

If a legitimate number is incorrectly identified as spam or miscategorized, visit our Verizon spam feedback page to let us know.

Verizon Protect app users with Call Filter can add phone numbers to a personal allowed list. Numbers added to this list will not be blocked*. Visit Add/remove allowed numbers to learn how.

*Some numbers may still be blocked by another app or service on your device. To help ensure any calls you want to receive are not blocked, you can also add them to your contacts.

How do I manage the spam calls that I receive?

Review full answer

Learn how to report and block a number as spam on our How to use the Verizon Protect app page.

What do the spam labels mean on my incoming call screen?

Review full answer

Potential Spam*: Unwanted callers that may be calling indiscriminately or inappropriately to a large number of recipients.

Robo Caller: Automated caller with a pre-recorded message.

Potential Fraud: An entity likely pretending to be someone they're not with malicious intent.

*All spam calls will be labeled as "Potential Spam" for customers enrolled in the free version of Call Filter.

What is the category filter in Call Filter Plus?

Review full answer

Verizon Call Filter Plus identifies calls by their category. You can block these calls by toggling the category filter in the Verizon Protect app.

Categories you can block are:

Telemarketer — sales call to solicit a product or service.

Nonprofit — a charity or other similar organization.

Political — a pre-recorded call to inform or inquire about political affiliations or upcoming elections.

Survey — an automated call to collect information.

Categories that can't be blocked are:

Healthcare — a doctor's office, hospital, ambulance, police station, etc.

Public Service — calling to address specific needs pertaining to members of a community.

Can Call Filter block calls from international numbers?

Review full answer

Yes, Call Filter can identify and block spam calls from international numbers the same way as domestic spam calls. You don't need to take additional action to block these calls if your spam filter is turned on.

What happens to calls that are blocked?

Review full answer

Spam calls will either go to voicemail or be terminated depending on what you select in the Verizon Protect app or My Verizon. If you do not update your settings, spam calls will go to voicemail by default.

What is Silence Junk Callers (iPhone only)?

Review full answer

If you are an iPhone user with iOS 14+ or newer, you have access to the Silence Junk Callers feature.

Silence Junk Callers is auto-enabled on your device.

It automatically silences potential fraud and spam calls and sends them to voicemail.

If a call is suspected as a spam call, you'll see "Maybe: Junk" on identified incoming calls.

Learn how to turn off Silence Junk Callers.

Keep in mind: Wanted calls may be inadvertently blocked when Silence Junk Callers is turned on.

What is the "Maybe" category that some of my calls are being identified with?

Review full answer

If you have iOS 17 or later, you may see certain calls (i.e., telemarketers, political, non-profits, survey) identified in a "Maybe" category.

If you want, you can use the Verizon Protect app to block these calls or send to voicemail. Just turn on Call Category blocking below the Spam Filter.

Keep in mind: Wanted calls may be inadvertently blocked when Silence Junk Callers is turned on.

Learn how to turn off Silence Junk Callers.

*Only available on iOS 14 and newer.

What is neighborhood spoofing and how is Verizon helping to protect me from these calls?

Stop neighborhood spoofing

Neighborhood Filter lets you automatically forwards calls from your area code/prefix to voicemail. Call Filter Plus lets you block entire area codes.

Call Filter blocks up to 5 area codes; Call Filter Plus blocks up to 10 area codes.

May inadvertently block some legitimate calls.

Review full answer

Neighborhood (or neighbor) spoofing is when a scammer calls you from a fake number that's familiar or similar to your own, to trick you into answering the call. The incoming call will have the same area code and likely the same 6-digit prefix as your own phone number, as scammers hope you'll think it's a neighbor.

To help protect you against neighbor spoofing calls, we've added Neighborhood Filter to the Verizon Protect app. With Neighborhood Filter you can automatically forward calls from your own area code/prefix and others to voicemail. With Call Filter Plus you can also filter out an entire area code.

Here's what you get:

Call Filter Plus area code options

Area code block

No

Up to 10
Yes*

Due to the complexity of neighborhood spoofing, all calls from area code/prefix or area code you entered will be blocked, not just spam calls. Some wanted calls may be inadvertently blocked.

Visit How to use the Verizon Protect app to learn how to use Neighborhood Filter.

*Area code block is only available on Android devices with the Verizon Protect app.

What if the neighborhood filter blocks a call I wanted to receive?

Review full answer

To make sure you receive calls from a number with an area code/prefix that you've blocked with Neighborhood Filter, save the number to your contacts or allow the number in the Verizon Protect app.

If the number is in your Recent Activity in the Verizon Protect app, you can select "Ignore filter" to unblock it.

Learn how to unblock numbers on the How to use the Verizon Protect app page.

Why should I use My Verizon to manage Call Filter instead of the Verizon Protect app on my device?

Review full answer

For certain devices*, the Verizon Protect app may not work. On these devices, it's best to use and manage Call Filter in My Verizon.

For select Verizon-purchased Android devices*, the Call Filter app can no longer be updated. As a result, some functionality (e.g., spam filter settings) may exist in the app but won't update to our systems or sync with My Verizon. On these devices, it's best to use and manage Call Filter in My Verizon.

Check if your device is or isn't compatible with the Verizon Protect app and if you can manage Call Filter in My Verizon.

Visit My Verizon Website - Manage Call Filter to learn how to turn off your spam filter or update your block preferences in My Verizon.

*Manage Call Filter by installing the Verizon Protect app. Your device must run Android 10.0+ or iOS 16.6+.

Why don't the Call Filter settings in My Verizon match my settings in the Verizon Protect app?

Review full answer

On a Verizon-purchased Android device, only the newest, most updated version of the Verizon Protect app will match the settings in My Verizon. To fix this, update* your Verizon Protect app in the Google Play Store.

*Eligible device required. In order to install the Verizon Protect app, you must have an Android phone with Android 10.0 or higher or an iPhone running iOS 16.6 or later.
