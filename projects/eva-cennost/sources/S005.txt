MegaFon's Virtual Assistant Eva Learns to Record and Save Phone Calls

Dialog recordings can be listened to and the audio file downloaded to your smartphone

Translated with AI from the Russian original.

MegaFon has launched a feature to record all incoming and outgoing calls. Now, subscribers can listen to or download audio files to their smartphones. This is especially convenient for those who frequently communicate by phone for work or personal purposes and want to save important conversations to clarify agreements.

Image source: Generated by DALL•E 3 neural network

The function can be enabled in the "Eva+" section. Once activated, all calls will be recorded automatically. You can also enable auto-informing the other party there. At the beginning of the conversation, participants will hear a notification about the recording.

Audio recordings are stored only in the MegaFon application, and only the number owner will have access to them. This guarantees complete confidentiality.

All recordings can be found in the special "Eva+" storage. It can hold up to 1 GB of files, which is equivalent to approximately 35 hours of conversations. If the total volume of audio files exceeds 1 GB or the storage period reaches 90 days, the oldest recordings will be deleted.

The service is free for all MegaFon subscribers with an "Eva+" subscription. Users from any region and with any smartphone operating systems can connect.
